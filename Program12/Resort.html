Paradise Resort

Nestled directly along the pristine, white-sand shores of South Bay, the resort offers an idyllic tropical retreat set against a backdrop of lush, palm-fringed coastal gardens. Designed for ultimate relaxation and convenience, the property features world-class facilities, including an infinity-edge pool, a full-service wellness spa, multiple fine-dining restaurants, and a state-of-the-art fitness center. Guests can easily explore top local attractions, from nearby coral reefs perfect for snorkeling and diving to vibrant seaside markets, historic lighthouses, and scenic coastal hiking trails.
